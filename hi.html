Wlecome to "Hang The Man" Game

Instructions

Please, be aware that this game is not for faint hearted. If you want to succeed you have to have at least basic knowledge of the countries and the capital cities of the Great continent of Africa. The game is played at least at this point, by YOU clicking on the available buttons, and if the letters are within the current country or city, that letter is displayed on the screen. In all other cases a part of the body will appear and if YOU have six wrong guesses of the letter, you're most probably be hanged!

Enjoy!
